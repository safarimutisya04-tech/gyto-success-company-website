Comprehensive Security Solutions

At GYTO Success Company, we recognise that no two clients have identical security needs. Every solution we design is carefully tailored to the specific requirements of your environment, sector, and risk profile.

Manned Guarding

Our manned guarding service is the cornerstone of what we do. We deploy professionally trained, uniformed security personnel to protect your premises, assets, and people around the clock. Every guard placed on your site has been through our rigorous recruitment, vetting, and training process.

We match the right personnel to each assignment, ensuring that our guards understand the specific culture, operations, and security requirements of your environment.

  • Static guarding for commercial and residential premises
  • Access control and visitor management
  • Perimeter security and patrol duties
  • Concierge and reception security
  • Retail loss prevention officers
  • Armed guard deployments (where licensed)

CCTV & Surveillance

Mobile Patrols

Our mobile patrol service provides a highly visible, cost-effective deterrent to criminal activity across a wide area. Marked patrol vehicles and uniformed officers carry out regular, unpredictable patrols of your site, neighbourhood, or estate to discourage threats before they materialise.

Mobile patrols are an excellent complement to static guarding or a standalone solution for clients who need broad coverage without a permanent on-site presence.

  • Scheduled and random patrol routes
  • Rapid-response to alarms and incidents
  • Lock-up and open-up services
  • Estate and residential area patrols
  • GPS-tracked patrol vehicles
  • Detailed patrol reports delivered to clients

K-9 Security Services

GYTO SUCCESS COMPANY LIMITED operates a professional K-9 (dog) security unit, deploying highly trained security dogs alongside certified handlers to provide an exceptional level of threat detection and deterrence. Our dogs are selected, trained, and certified to the highest standards, making them an invaluable asset for protecting high-value premises, perimeters, and people.

Security dogs offer a natural and powerful deterrent that technology alone cannot replicate. Their acute senses of smell and hearing make them highly effective at detecting intruders, narcotics, explosives, and other contraband � day or night, in any terrain.

German Shepherd

Highly intelligent, loyal, and versatile � ideal for patrol, detection, and guard duties. The most widely deployed breed in professional security worldwide.

Rottweiler

Powerful, confident, and deeply loyal to their handlers. Rottweilers are excellent for perimeter patrol and active deterrence on large or high-risk sites.

Belgian Malinois

Agile, fast, and highly trainable � favoured by military and law enforcement agencies globally for detection, search-and-rescue, and rapid response operations.

Doberman Pinscher

Alert, energetic, and fearless. Dobermans excel at night patrol and close-protection roles, offering a striking visual deterrent while remaining acutely sensitive to threats.

  • Trained patrol and guard dogs with certified handlers
  • Explosive and narcotics detection dogs
  • Perimeter and estate patrol units
  • Event security and crowd deterrence
  • Night-time and low-visibility patrols
  • Rapid-response dog units for alarm activations
