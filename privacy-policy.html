1. Who We Are

GYTO Success Company Limited ("GYTO", "we", "us", or "our") is a Kenyan-registered security services company with offices at Junction of Kandara and Gatundu Road, opposite Kenya High School, Nairobi, Kenya. We operate this website (gytosecurity.co.ke) to provide information about our services, accept enquiries, and receive job applications.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and handling it responsibly in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and any applicable regulations issued thereunder.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information in the following circumstances:

2.1 Contact Enquiry Form

When you submit a message through our Contact Us page, we collect:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Company or organisation name (optional)
  • Service of interest
  • The content of your message

2.2 Recruitment Application Form

When you submit a job application through our Recruitment page, we collect:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Details of two referees (name and phone number)
  • Details of two next-of-kin contacts (name and phone number)
  • Uploaded documents including: passport photo, National ID copy, KRA PIN certificate, NSSF card, NHIF card, bank account details, academic certificates, police clearance certificate, medical certificate, letter from chief, sketch map, and application letter

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect basic technical information such as your browser type, device type, and general geographic location. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools that collect personally identifiable information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry or request for a security assessment or quotation
  • To process and evaluate your job application
  • To contact you regarding your enquiry or application via email or phone
  • To maintain a record of communications for service quality purposes
  • To comply with our legal obligations under Kenyan law

We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases as provided under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019:

  • Consent — you voluntarily provide your information by submitting a form on our website
  • Contractual necessity — processing is necessary to respond to your service request or evaluate your employment application
  • Legitimate interests — maintaining records of communications for operational and legal purposes
  • Legal obligation — where we are required by law to retain or disclose information

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within GYTO Success Company Limited — relevant staff who need the information to respond to your enquiry or process your application
  • Legal requirements — if we are required to disclose information by law, court order, or a government authority in Kenya
  • Business transfer — in the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of the company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction

We do not transfer your personal data outside of Kenya except where required by law or where you have explicitly consented.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Enquiries — retained for up to 2 years from the date of submission to allow follow-up communication
  • Job applications (unsuccessful) — retained for 6 months after the recruitment process is concluded, then securely deleted
  • Job applications (successful) — retained for the duration of employment and for 7 years thereafter in accordance with Kenyan employment law

After the applicable retention period, personal data is permanently deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to erasure — you may request that we delete your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected
  • Right to object — you may object to the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 12 below. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include:

  • Secure HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Restricted access to personal data — only authorised staff can access submitted information
  • Password-protected admin systems for managing submitted enquiries and applications
  • Regular review of our data handling practices

While we take these precautions, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

9. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking technologies. We use only essential browser storage (localStorage) to temporarily save form submissions on the device used to access the site. This data is never transmitted to external servers and remains solely on your device.

You may clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or browsing data.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information through our website, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete that information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
