Who We Are

GYTO Success Company Limited is a medium-sized and Kenyan-owned business. We have been in the security industry for over 27 years, making us an interpersonal and experienced organisation with an ethos to match.

Our culture transcends from the very top directors to employees on the ground securing our clients. We believe that real security begins with real relationships � knowing our clients, understanding their environments, and building tailor-made solutions that genuinely protect them.

We have a close, open, and mutually beneficial relationship with all our customers that generates deep and lasting trust. This is not just a service we offer � it is the foundation of everything we do.

Mission, Vision & Purpose

Guided by clear principles that shape every decision we make and every service we deliver.

Our Mission

To provide integrated security solutions through delivery of quality services by employing a consulting approach in the management of our customers' risk exposure.

Our Vision

To lead in security provision to both individual and corporate clients in Kenya and the rest of the world.

Our Purpose

We exist because of our trusted customers. We are committed to continuously elevating the safety status of Kenya, one client at a time, by building relationships that last a lifetime.

Our Core Values

The principles that drive our culture and define how we show up for every client, every day.

Trust

We build every client relationship on a foundation of honesty, transparency, and consistent delivery. When we commit, we follow through � no exceptions.

Integrity

Our conduct is guided by the highest ethical standards, both in our interactions with clients and in the way we manage and treat our staff on the ground.

Excellence

We refuse to be average. From training our guards to maintaining equipment, we constantly pursue excellence in everything we do.

People-First

Our people are our greatest asset � both our clients and our employees. We invest in their growth, welfare, and development to deliver consistently superior service.

Innovation

We embrace new technologies and methods to stay ahead of evolving security threats, continuously improving our capabilities to better serve our clients.

Patriotism

As a proudly Kenyan-owned company, we are committed to contributing positively to our nation's safety, economy, and development through our work every day.

Our Company Ethos

At GYTO Success Company Limited, security is more than a business � it is a calling. Our leadership team brings decades of combined experience in the Kenyan security landscape, and they remain personally involved in the delivery of our services.

The ethos of our business transcends from the very top directors to employees on the ground. Every guard who wears the GYTO uniform is a reflection of our values. We invest heavily in training, welfare, and professional development to ensure that our team can perform to the highest standard under any circumstance.

Our interpersonal approach means we get to know our clients deeply � understanding their specific vulnerabilities, routines, and risk profiles � so we can design security plans that truly work.
